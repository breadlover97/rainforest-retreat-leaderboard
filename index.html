❓ Campaign Archive FAQ

Can I still enter or earn ballot chances?

No. Entries closed on 31 July 2026 and this campaign has ended.

Will these standings continue to update?

No. Automatic updates have stopped. The date at the top of this page shows when the preserved leaderboard snapshot was generated.

Are these the prize winners?

No. This table records ballot standings only. It does not confirm official draw results or prize fulfilment. Refer to the organiser's original announcement for those details.

How were ballot chances earned during the campaign?

Completing a Financial Clarity Session earned 1 ballot. Successful referrals earned additional ballots according to the tiers above, after the referred person completed their session. The same rules applied to existing clients and non-clients.

Why is the draw tool still available?

It is retained for historical verification using the original frozen ballot file and recorded seed. A replay on this site does not create a new official draw or independently verify the original inputs.
